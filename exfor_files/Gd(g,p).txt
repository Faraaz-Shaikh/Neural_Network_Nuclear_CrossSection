TITLE      Photonuclear quadrupole resonance in 160Gd.
AUTHOR     (F.Dreyer,H.Dahmen,J.Staube,H.H.Thies)
INSTITUTE  (2GERHEI)
REFERENCE  (J,NP/A,192,433,1972)
FACILITY   (BETAT,2GERHEI) Heidelberg 33 MeV betatron.
INC-SOURCE (BRST)
METHOD     (ACTIV)
SAMPLE     Samples of 99.9 % pure 160Gd.
DETECTOR   (IOCH) Ionization chamber.
ANALYSIS   (LEAST) Cook least-structure method.
ERR-ANALYS (DATA-ERR) Mean-root-squared unsertainties.
COMMENT    The (g,n) and  (g,p)  cross  section  in  160Gd  were
            measured from    threshold    to    33   MeV   using
            bremsstrahlung from 33 MeV betatron and observing the
            induced activities.  Both  cross  sections contain a
            broad maximum at 23.5 MeV.  It is argued  that  this
            maximum is the quadrupole resonance predicted at 1.6
            times the  E1  giant   resonance   energy   by   the
            hydrodinamic model.
