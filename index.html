1D Particle in a Box

Last updated: Dec 2025 • Reading time: 5 mins

The infinite square well is one of the simplest non-trivial problems in quantum mechanics. It demonstrates quantization of energy and the formation of standing waves.

The Hamiltonian

Consider a particle of mass $m$ trapped in a 1D box of length $L$. The potential $V(x)$ is defined as:

$$ V(x) = \begin{cases} 0 & 0 < x < L \\ \infty & \text{otherwise} \end{cases} $$

Inside the well ($V=0$), the Time-Independent Schrödinger Equation (TISE) simplifies to:

$$ -\frac{\hbar^2}{2m} \frac{d^2\psi}{dx^2} = E\psi $$

Physics Note: The boundary conditions $\psi(0) = \psi(L) = 0$ force the wave numbers to be quantized: $k_n = \frac{n\pi}{L}$.
